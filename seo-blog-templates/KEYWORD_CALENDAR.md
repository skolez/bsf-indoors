## Keyword Research Sources

- Google Keyword Planner (free with Google Ads account)
- Google Trends (seasonal patterns)
- Google autocomplete + "People also ask"
- Reddit/forums in your niche (find real questions people ask)
- Competitor blogs (what keywords are they ranking for?)
- Amazon search suggestions (product-focused keywords)
