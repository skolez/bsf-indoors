## Editorial cadence

Per the portfolio roadmap (`../farmable-insects/README.md` Phase 1):

- **Launch threshold:** 10–15 articles published before promoting the site
- **Pre-launch capture:** photos and notes from every cycle of the colony — they become the article archive
- **Cadence after launch:** 1–2 articles/week for the first 8 weeks, then 1/week sustained

## Source-grounding requirement

Every article in Tiers 1, 2, and 4 must cite at least **two sources** from
`agents/sources.md`. Tier 3 may cite one. The site's editorial promise is
"firsthand + cited" — drift on either side is a content-quality red flag.
