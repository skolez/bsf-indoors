# SEO Blog Templates

This directory contains templates and guides for creating SEO-optimized affiliate blog content for {{SITE_NAME}}.

## Contents

- **CONTENT_GUIDE.md** — Complete content style guide, SEO standards, and E-E-A-T requirements
- **templates/** — Seven ready-to-use blog post templates

## Quick Start

1. **Read CONTENT_GUIDE.md** first — It covers voice, SEO fundamentals, and quality standards
2. **Choose a template** based on your keyword and content type
3. **Follow the template structure** — It includes placeholders, word count targets, and markup examples
4. **Check the quality checklist** in CONTENT_GUIDE.md before publishing

## Blog Template Types

1. **best-x-for-y.md** — Product roundup (highest affiliate revenue)
2. **product-review.md** — Single product review
3. **comparison-post.md** — X vs Y comparisons
4. **how-to-guide.md** — Step-by-step tutorials
5. **buyers-guide.md** — Comprehensive pillar pages
6. **seasonal-guide.md** — Timely, seasonal content
7. **problem-solution.md** — Troubleshooting and solutions

## SEO Strategy Overview

- **Target keywords:** Primary keyword per post + 2-5 secondary keywords
- **Regional focus:** Optimize for {{REGION}} where relevant
- **Audience:** {{AUDIENCE}}
- **E-E-A-T:** Author credentials, sources, "last updated" dates, affiliate disclosure
- **Internal linking:** 2-4 blog links + 1-3 store links per post
- **Affiliate density:** Max 1 link per 200-300 words
- **Content length:** Varies by type (see CONTENT_GUIDE for specifics)

## Publishing Workflow

1. **Select template** based on keyword research
2. **Fill in metadata** (title tag, meta description, primary keyword)
3. **Write content** using template structure
4. **Add schema markup** (Article, FAQ, Product, HowTo as applicable)
5. **Link internally** to related blog posts and store pages
6. **Run quality checklist** from CONTENT_GUIDE.md
7. **Publish and share** (update keyword calendar, internal linking map, etc.)
8. **Update quarterly** — Refresh dates, prices, rankings

## Maintaining Blog Content

- **Monthly:** Check top-performing posts, update data/prices, refresh "last updated" dates
- **Quarterly:** Audit internal links for breaks, consolidate overlapping content
- **Annually:** Comprehensive review of blog structure, keyword performance, conversion rates

## Integration with Knowledge Base

Blog posts reference and link to knowledge-base files in `../knowledge-base/`:

- Use KB as source material for blog posts
- Link from blog posts to related KB content
- Keep KB updated when discovering better information through research
- Log any source conflicts in `../agents/conflicts.md`

## Customization

All templates use {{PLACEHOLDER}} values that should be customized for your niche:

- {{SITE_NAME}} — Your website/brand name
- {{NICHE}} — Your content focus (e.g., beekeeping, woodworking, gardening)
- {{AUDIENCE}} — Your target reader (e.g., beginners, homeowners)
- {{REGION}} — Geographic focus (e.g., Cache Valley, Northern Utah)
- {{PRODUCT}} — Specific items you're reviewing
- [Additional placeholders] — See individual templates

Replace all {{PLACEHOLDER}} values with your actual content before publishing.
