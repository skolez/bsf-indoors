# Template: Comparison Post (X vs Y)

> Use this template for decision-stage keywords like "[Product A] vs [Product B]"
> or "[Type 1] vs [Type 2]". These posts capture readers actively comparing
> options and are high-converting for affiliate revenue.

---

## Target Metrics
- **Word count:** 1,500–2,500
- **Products/options compared:** 2 primary + 1–3 alternatives
- **Affiliate links:** 3–5
- **Internal links:** 2–4 (including related reviews and roundups)
- **Time to read:** 7–10 minutes

## Key Principles

1. **Be fair to both** — Don't write a hatchet job. Acknowledge strengths and weaknesses of each.
2. **Lead with the verdict** — Readers want to know the answer quickly.
3. **Use a comparison table** — Visual comparison is crucial for decision-stage content.
4. **Reference individual reviews** — Link to full reviews of each product.
5. **Consider use cases** — Different products win for different users; make that clear.
6. **Include regional context** — How do they perform in {{REGION}}?
7. **Answer the question** — The title asks a question; your post should answer it decisively.
8. **Update regularly** — Refresh prices, availability, and "last updated" date quarterly.
