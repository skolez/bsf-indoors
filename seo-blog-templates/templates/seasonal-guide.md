# Template: Seasonal / Timely Guide

> Use this template for seasonal content tied to your {{NICHE}} calendar. These
> posts perform well year after year (if you update them), capture seasonal
> searches, and drive affiliate sales during peak buying seasons.

---

## Target Metrics
- **Word count:** 2,000–3,000
- **Products/recommendations:** 3–8
- **Affiliate links:** 4–8
- **Internal links:** 3–5 (related seasonal guides, how-to posts, buying guides)
- **Time to read:** 10–15 minutes
- **Publishing timing:** Publish 2–4 weeks before the season begins

- **After the season:** Archive specific dates for next year's refresh

## Key Principles

1. **Publish ahead of time** — Release 2–4 weeks before the season to capture preparation searches.
2. **Be {{REGION}}-specific** — Seasonal timing varies dramatically by climate. Lock in {{REGION}} dates.
3. **Update annually** — Refresh every year with current products, pricing, and any method changes.
4. **Link to related content** — Connect to how-to guides, reviews, and buying guides for {{SEASON}} products.
5. **Provide actionable tasks** — Give readers specific dates and actions they can take.
6. **Anticipate challenges** — Include troubleshooting section for common {{SEASON}} problems.
7. **Encourage action** — Use a checklist or clear summary to motivate {{AUDIENCE}} to prepare.
