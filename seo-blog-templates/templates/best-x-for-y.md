# Template: "Best X for Y" Roundup Post

> This is the highest-converting affiliate content type. Use it for keywords
> like "best [product] for [audience]," "best [product] for [use case]," etc.
> These posts capture commercial-intent searchers ready to buy.

---

## Target Metrics
- **Word count:** 2,500–4,000
- **Products featured:** 5–9 (sweet spot is 7)
- **Affiliate links:** 7–15
- **Internal links:** 3–5
- **Time to read:** 10–15 minutes

## Key Principles

1. **Lead with the winner** — Don't make readers scroll. Show your #1 pick in the comparison table AND in the intro.
2. **Differentiate picks** — Each product should have a unique "Best for" angle (budget, premium, beginners, advanced, specific use case).
3. **Be honest about cons** — Every product has drawbacks. Listing them builds trust.
4. **Show your work** — Mention specific testing details, not vague "we tried lots of products."
5. **Update regularly** — Include "Last updated" date. Review and refresh at least every 6 months.
6. **Regional angle** — At least 2–3 mentions of how products perform in {{REGION}} / {{CLIMATE_ZONE}} conditions.
7. **Link internally** — Connect to related how-to guides, seasonal content, or comparison posts for {{NICHE}}.
