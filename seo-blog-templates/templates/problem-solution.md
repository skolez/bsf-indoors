# Template: Problem / Solution (Troubleshooting)

> Use this template for pain-point and troubleshooting keywords like "why is
> [problem happening]" or "[problem] solutions". These posts capture readers
> at their moment of frustration and drive both affiliate sales and trust.

---

## Target Metrics
- **Word count:** 1,500–2,500
- **Solutions provided:** 3–7 main remedies
- **Affiliate links:** 2–5 (products that solve the problem)
- **Internal links:** 2–4 (related how-to guides, reviews, problem-solution posts)
- **Time to read:** 7–10 minutes

## Key Principles

1. **Lead with empathy** — Reader is frustrated. Acknowledge that.
2. **Offer quick wins first** — Start with fast solutions, then go deeper.
3. **Escalate complexity** — Move from quick fixes to comprehensive solutions to prevention.
4. **Be clear about trade-offs** — Every solution has pros and cons; be honest.
5. **Link products smartly** — Products should genuinely solve the problem, not feel forced.
6. **Include {{REGION}} context** — Does this problem manifest differently in {{REGION}}?
7. **Give action steps** — Make solutions implementable, with clear steps or links to how-to guides.
8. **Know when to defer** — Be honest about when {{AUDIENCE}} should get professional help.
