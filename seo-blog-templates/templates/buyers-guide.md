# Template: Buyer's Guide / Pillar Page

> Use this template for comprehensive, hub-style content targeting broad keywords
> like "[category] buying guide" or "complete guide to [topic]". These are pillar
> pages that should link to multiple cluster posts and drive significant organic
> traffic and affiliate revenue.

---

## Target Metrics
- **Word count:** 3,000–5,000+ (comprehensive coverage)
- **Products/options featured:** 5–15
- **Affiliate links:** 10–20

- **Time to read:** 15–25 minutes
- **Sections:** 8–12 major sections

## Key Principles

1. **Comprehensive but organized** — Cover everything, but use clear sections so readers can jump to what matters.
2. **Link extensively** — This is a pillar page. Link to all relevant cluster posts (reviews, comparisons, how-tos).
3. **Feature diverse options** — Include budget, premium, and specialty picks to serve different readers.
4. **Include {{REGION}} context** — How does this topic apply in {{REGION}}?
5. **Anticipate questions** — FAQ section should answer the 7–10 most common questions.
6. **Update regularly** — This is a cornerstone page. Refresh quarterly or when major changes occur.
7. **Lead with E-E-A-T** — Establish authority early; cite sources; show first-hand experience.
8. **Use data and visuals** — Tables, images, and structured comparisons help readers scan and decide.
