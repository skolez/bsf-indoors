_No conflicts logged yet. This file tracks disagreements between sources discovered during enrichment._

## Format
When logging conflicts, use this format:

- **Topic:** What the conflict is about
- **Source A:** [Source name] says X
- **Source B:** [Source name] says Y
- **Resolution:** Which source is more authoritative and why
- **Action:** What was done (updated KB, flagged for review, created clarification, etc.)

## How to Log a Conflict

1. When you discover two sources with conflicting information during Phase 1b enrichment or Phase 2 distillation, add a new conflict entry
2. Be specific about the claim (quote or paraphrase the exact disagreement)
3. Research the sources' credentials and recency
4. Document which source you chose as authoritative and why
5. Note what action you took (update KB, flag for review, create note in relevant KB file, etc.)
6. Use consistent formatting for easy scanning

This log helps maintain transparency about knowledge base decisions and creates a revision history for fact-checking.
