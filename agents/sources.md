## Tier 1 — Peer-reviewed and authoritative

1. **Tomberlin & Sheppard 2010 — Artificial Light Source Influences Mating and Oviposition of *Hermetia illucens*** (J. Insect Science) [L,C]
   - https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3029228/
   - Foundational paper. Quartz-iodine lamp 61% of sunlight mating rate; rare-earth lamp = 0%.
   - RAG 3 / Reg 3 / Aud 3 — universal

2. **Tomberlin & Sheppard 2010 (Oxford mirror)** [L,C]
   - https://academic.oup.com/jinsectscience/article/10/1/202/891652
   - Same study, journal-of-record link.

3. **Oonincx et al. 2018 — Influence of three artificial light sources on oviposition and half-life of BSF: Improving small-scale indoor rearing** (PLOS ONE) [L,C]
   - https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5968407/
   - Title says "small-scale indoor rearing" — directly addresses this site's audience.
   - RAG 3 / Reg 3 / Aud 3

4. **Hoc et al. 2019 — Photoreceptor spectral sensitivity informing LED-based illumination** (J. Insect Physiology) [L]
   - https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0022191016301457
   - UV + blue + green sensitivity; full 350–700 nm range needed. Justifies why halogen-with-UV-pass works.
   - RAG 3 / Reg 2 / Aud 2

5. **Hoc et al. 2020 — Mating success under four artificial light sources** (J. Asia-Pacific Entomology) [L]
   - https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32092662/
   - Head-to-head bulb comparison.
   - RAG 3 / Reg 3 / Aud 3

6. **Macavei et al. 2024 — Heterogeny in cages: age-structure and attractant timing** (bioRxiv preprint) [C]
   - https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.08.13.607807v1.full
   - When to introduce oviposition substrate relative to adult age.
   - RAG 2 / Reg 3 / Aud 2 — preprint, flag

7. **Bertinetti et al. 2022 — Larval Locomotion in BSF, with a Novel "Self-Harvesting" Method** (Insects, MDPI) [R]
   - https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8879302/
   - The dedicated paper on prepupa locomotion. Migration triggered by low light + high moisture; 45° ramp angle works.
   - RAG 3 / Reg 3 / Aud 3

8. **Sheppard et al. 2002 — Rearing Methods for the Black Soldier Fly** (J. Medical Entomology) [C,F]
   - https://academic.oup.com/jme/article/39/4/695/904029
   - Canonical rearing paper. T 27 °C, RH 70%, oviposition crevice substrates.
   - RAG 3 / Reg 3 / Aud 2

9. **Tomberlin & Sheppard 2002 — Factors Influencing Mating and Oviposition in a Colony** [C]
   - https://www.researchgate.net/publication/286157351
   - T, RH, density, age effects on mating.

10. **Park et al. 2020 — Mating Success and Reproductive Behavior in Tropics** (J. Entomology) [C]
    - https://scialert.net/fulltext/?doi=je.2020.117.127
    - Tropical baseline; useful contrast case.

11. **Booth & Sheppard — Ovitrap Preference in BSF** [C]
    - https://scialert.net/fulltext/?doi=pjbs.2021.562.570
    - What attracts females. Direct input for cardboard oviposition strips.

12. **Pang et al. 2024 — Hidden drivers: density, moisture, scale** (PLOS ONE) [F]
    - https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0317049

13. **Cammack & Tomberlin — Rearing substrate impacts growth (industrial scale)** (Scientific Reports) [F]
    - https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-76571-8

14. **Lalander et al. — Effect of rearing substrate on growth, waste reduction, composition** [F]
    - https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29752718/

15. **Gold et al. 2024 — Use of different dry materials to control moisture in BSF substrate** [F]
    - https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38560477/
    - Direct evidence on aspen / coir / sawdust additions — exactly the bin-substrate question.

16. **Discover Animals (Springer) 2024 — Optimizing substrate moisture for larval survival** [F]
    - https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44338-024-00005-2
    - 75% moisture optimum on spent-mushroom + chicken-feed mix.

17. **PLOS ONE — Biowaste and by-products as rearing substrates: effects on body composition** [F]
    - https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0275213

18. **Barragán-Fonseca et al. — Physical properties of substrates as a growth driver** [F]
    - https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10054678/

19. **PLOS ONE — Comprehensive dynamic growth and development model of BSF larvae** [F]
    - https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0239084

20. **Diener et al. — Small-scale rearing in the laboratory: low-cost, year-round** [C,F]
    - https://www.researchgate.net/publication/283746445
    - "Year-round" + "low-cost" + "laboratory" = cornerstone source for an indoor cold-climate site.

21. **US Patent App. 20150122182 — Contained Systems to Provide Reproductive Habitat for *Hermetia illucens*** [L,C]
    - https://patents.justia.com/patent/20150122182

22. **UF/IFAS EENY-461 — Black Soldier Fly fact sheet** (Univ. of Florida Extension) [C,F]

## Tier 2 — Industry / commercial protocols

31. **EVO Conversion Systems — Science Blog** [L,C,R,F]
    - http://www.evoconsys.com/blog
    - Tomberlin-affiliated company; technical posts on production.

31a. **EVO Conversion Systems — SPR AgTech BSF Mating Light** [L]
    - http://www.evoconsys.com/products.html
    - Production-grade BSF-tuned multi-channel LED ($180, ordered 2026-04-26 by site operator). Direct commercial implementation of Tomberlin & Sheppard 2010 (#1) + Hoc 2019/2020 (#4, #5) photoreceptor work. UVA + blue + green channels matched to BSF compound-eye sensitivity. Multi-thousand-hour rated, runs cool, no fingerprint failure mode.
    - Treated as a Tier 2 source because it's a commercial product, but the underlying spec is Tier 1 research. Will be the gold-standard reference once on-rig data accumulates.
    - Scores: RAG 3 / Reg 3 / Aud 3

32. **EnviroFlight (Marysville, KY)** [F]

33. **Symton Black Soldier Fly — Intensive BSF Farming blog post** [L,C,R,F]
